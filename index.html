UZI (from Murder Drones)

UZI is great at a few things: being chaotic, being brave, and saving people when it matters most.

Character story

Uzi's dad is a doorman; he builds doors to keep out the Murder Drones. One day, Uzi decides she wants to go outside and fight them. She builds a gun and opens the door that had been keeping everyone safe. This lets the Murder Drones inside, and several people die. By the end, they are not friends, but they are not enemies either.

Achievements and goals

Achievements

  • She saves her dad and classmates from the Murder Drones.
  • At the end, she defeats Cyn and saves Copper-9 from destruction.

Goals

  • She begins by wanting to explore the outside world.
  • Her goals shift when she meets an unexpected friend.
  • She ultimately wants peace and a future with her partner.

Accomplishments

  • She hacks the memories of V and N, saving them from Cyn's plans.
  • In the final battle, she gains full control over the Absolute Solver.
